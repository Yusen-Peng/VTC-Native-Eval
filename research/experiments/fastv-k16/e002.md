## Interpretation
Interim (20/23 cells). At equal cost (within 0.2 % TF), FastV-16 is >= PyramidDrop-16 on 19 of the 20 finished
cells; the exception is 2x MMMU (47.56 vs 47.77, -0.21, noise). Smallest positive margins: 8x ChartQA +0.4, 8x MMMU +0.9. At 2x the OCR gap to PDrop-16
is large (TextVQA +5.4, DocVQA +4.2, OCRBench +10.4, ChartQA +14.6), so the gradual multi-stage schedule is not
what makes PDrop-16 work on NEO; the later first pruning layer is, and a single prune at K=16 does better. The
counter-argument (8x one-shot prune collapses OCR) does not hold so far: 8x OCRBench 15.7 vs 8.5, 8x MMB 69.85 vs 58.16.
FastV-2 keeps an edge only on ChartQA (2x 79.2 vs 77.6, 4x 61.5 vs 53.8, 8x 42.4 vs 24.0) at 0.31-0.70x the cost.
Backlog 4 condition (within 2 pts of PDrop-16 at 2B 2x, or better) is met, so e003 (K=24) is submitted now
rather than after the last three cells.
