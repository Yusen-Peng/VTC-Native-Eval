## Hypothesis
Measurement behind the e007 interpretation. The warm-started paper-faithful `learned` module at step 30 emits, like the
collapsed random-init module (e001), the same vector at every output position (a global image summary on top of the
image-independent latent grid), because its attention, copied from the pooled-2x module that never used its attention
branch, is near-uniform over the native tokens and `o_proj` therefore adds one mean-value vector to every query. If so,
the LLM under its step-30 adapter reads a small global signal (trained below zeros) but the module carries no per-position
content, and the loss equals the collapsed module's on the same samples.
