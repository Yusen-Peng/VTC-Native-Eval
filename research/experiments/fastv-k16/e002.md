## Hypothesis
Moving FastV's single pruning step from layer 2 to layer 16 recovers most of the OCR gap to PyramidDrop-16 at
equal cost, because at K=2 NEO's last-token attention has seen raw patch embeddings for only two layers and ranks
image tokens almost by position.
