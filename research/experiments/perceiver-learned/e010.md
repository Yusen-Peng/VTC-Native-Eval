## Hypothesis
Backlog item 3b, the cheap test e008 asked for. e008 measured why the warm-started paper-faithful module (e005/e007) stays on
the collapsed curve: its attention becomes position-selective within 30 steps (entropy 0.11 of log N), but the output is
`norm_out(latents + o_proj(attn))` with a latent grid that is 82% one common direction at native RMS (4.52), so the
image-dependent attention branch (o_proj grown from zero to 38% of a trained module's scale by step 30) is a few percent of
every token and the LLM receives one shared vector plus an 11% perturbation (output pairwise cosine 0.994). If that shared
direction is the obstacle, removing it from the init (latents = the 18% spatial part only, RMS 0.82 instead of 4.52, the
mean cell vector subtracted) lets the attention branch become the dominant per-position content within the first tens of
steps, and the loss leaves the collapsed curve where e007 did not. If the loss again tracks the collapsed curve over steps
25-37, the shared direction was not the obstacle and the paper-faithful architecture stays closed at this data scale with
no remaining cheap variant.
