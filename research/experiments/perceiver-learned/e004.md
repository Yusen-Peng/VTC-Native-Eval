## Interpretation
The e002 fix carries to 4x: no collapse (loss 0.897 vs the collapsed 1.096, MMBench 66.9 vs 24.1), and the row is pooled-like
on every benchmark. Unlike 2x, where learned_residual and pooled were the same row within noise, at 4x learned_residual is
above pooled on 7 of 8 benchmarks (+2.3 MMBench, +37 MME, +2.0 DocVQA, +1.1 TextVQA, +0.8 OCRBench, +0.8 ChartQA, +0.7 RWQA;
MMMU -0.7 = 6 items) and the paired loss gap grew from -0.012 (2x) to -0.043 (4x, steady -0.033 with sd 0.011 over the last 163
steps), so the gain is real and grows with compression. What produces it is not settled by this run: e003 showed that at 2x
the latents are inert and the whole departure from fixed pooling is the per-token MLP branch, which moves because the compressor
lr is 1e-3 instead of the pooled row's 2e-5. The 4x gain is therefore most likely a learning-rate effect on the pooled
architecture (the MLP branch has more to correct when each token summarises 4 patches), not a learned-query effect; the exact
control is a `pooled` run at compressor lr 1e-3 (e009). For `tab:training-based` the 4x learned-query Perceiver row is
66.92 / 1408.4 / 44.00 / 52.03 / 16.77 / 16.23 / 3.6 / 22.36, with the same footnote as the 2x row (pooled-anchored queries,
latents inert at 2x by e003; not re-probed at 4x).
