## Interpretation
The warm-started paper-faithful module is, step for step, the collapsed random-init module: over steps 25-37 the two losses
differ by 0.004 nats with a paired sd of 0.007 (the batch-to-batch sd of the losses themselves is about 0.07), i.e. the LLM
receives the same information from both, which e001 measured as one global image vector (0.27 nats over zeros) and nothing
per position. The weight check says why "warm" did not help: the only parts of the init that could make the output
position-dependent are the attention weights, and those were copied from the trained pooled-2x module, whose attention is
near-uniform (e001: entropy 0.957 of log N, max weight 0.005) because the pooled run never used its attention branch (gate
0.004). Uniform attention makes every query read the same mean value vector, so `o_proj` (now at 38% of trained scale)
adds one global vector to every position, exactly the e001 mechanism, with the RMS problem of cause (b) removed. In other
words, cause (b) "scale at init" was necessary for the random-init collapse but not sufficient to explain the family: an
image-independent query grid needs its attention to become position-selective through q/k gradients, and in 39 steps at lr
1e-3 those moved by 30% of their scale without the loss moving at all. The pooled anchor (e002) sidesteps this because the
pooled tokens already carry per-position content, which is why that row trains and this one does not. e008 measures the
step-30 module directly (attention entropy, output cosine, loss under zeros/noise) to replace this inference with numbers.
Backlog item 3 is closed: the paper-faithful architecture does not train from a good init at this data scale (66K samples,
488-step schedule); the table's "Perceiver, learned queries" rows are the `learned_residual` runs with the e003 footnote.
