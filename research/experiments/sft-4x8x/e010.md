## Interpretation
At 4x the 2x pattern holds and sharpens. (a) LoRA SFT on LLaVA@66K recovers general VQA, and more of it than at 2x
because there is more to recover: fixed +23.0 MMBench (2x: +3.8), random +6.8 (2x: -0.5), bringing both within 2-5 pts
of ToMe 4x. OCR does not move: DocVQA +3.4 / +2.8, TextVQA +3.8 / +0.8, ChartQA +2.2, so SFT teaches the LLM to use the
pooled embeddings for coarse semantics but cannot restore glyph-level detail that pooling or pruning destroyed.
(b) DRIP keeps a large OCR advantage over fixed/random+SFT (+38.7 DocVQA, +22 TextVQA/ChartQA, +6 OCRBench) but loses
ground to ToMe faster than at 2x: DocVQA gap to ToMe -6.1 at 2x, -11.1 at 4x; ChartQA -12.1 -> -29.2; OCRBench -4.4 -> -5.0,
with DRIP OCRBench collapsing 40.6 -> 11.6. That fits the counter-argument in the Theory: part of
the 2x DRIP OCR score comes from the half of the tokens kept intact, and the learned boundaries do not track text
lines well enough to replace that at 4x. Backlog item 3 (DRIP below random+SFT on DocVQA) is not triggered.
Caveat: single seed, single 488-step run per cell; differences under ~2 pts on MMBench/MMMU are inside run noise.
