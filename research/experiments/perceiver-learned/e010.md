## Hypothesis
Backlog item 3b, the cheap test e008 asked for. e008 measured why the warm-started paper-faithful module (e005/e007) stays on
the collapsed curve: its attention becomes position-selective within 30 steps (entropy 0.11 of log N), but the output is
`norm_out(latents + o_proj(attn))` with a latent grid that is 82% one common direction at native RMS (4.52), so the
image-dependent attention branch (o_proj grown from zero to 38% of a trained module's scale by step 30) is a few percent of
every token and the LLM receives one shared vector plus an 11% perturbation (output pairwise cosine 0.994). If that shared
direction is the obstacle, removing it from the init (latents = the 18% spatial part only, RMS 0.82 instead of 4.52, the
mean cell vector subtracted) lets the attention branch become the dominant per-position content within the first tens of
steps, and the loss leaves the collapsed curve where e007 did not. If the loss again tracks the collapsed curve over steps
25-37, the shared direction was not the obstacle and the paper-faithful architecture stays closed at this data scale with
no remaining cheap variant.

## Interpretation
Removing the shared latent direction did not free the attention branch; it made the init worse and training restored the
collapsed state within three steps. Three things follow. (1) The mean native-token direction that e008 found dominating the
warm grid is what made the warm init tolerable to the frozen LLM (loss 1.35 at step 1 with it, 5.35 without it): it is the
LLM's prior for "a visual token", not an obstacle. (2) The collapsed level (1.15-1.18 over steps 11-37, the same for the random
init, the warm init and the centred warm init, three different starting points) is an attractor of this training setup, not an
artefact of any one init: whatever the paper-faithful module starts from, after 10 warmup steps it emits what the e001/e008
probes measured, one global image vector that the LLM reads for 0.27-0.35 nats and nothing per position. The probe makes this a
measurement: with the latents pairwise-orthogonal at step 0 and the outputs pairwise-orthogonal (cosine 0.0001), the module
reaches output cosine 0.9995 by step 30 through its attention/o_proj path alone (the queries are still orthogonal). The
"collapse" is therefore what gradient descent on this LLM's loss does to any image-independent query grid in the first steps:
the frozen LLM cannot read S unfamiliar per-position vectors (loss 5.07 on the centred init) and the fastest loss reduction is to
make every output the one vector the LLM tolerates, after which per-position content has no gradient. (3) The pooled anchor
(e002) is therefore not "a better init of the same thing" but a different optimisation problem: its per-position content exists
at step 0 and the LLM's gradient to the module is informative from the first step, which the e003 probe showed the module then
barely uses (the gains are the compressor lr). Backlog item 3 is closed in all its forms: the paper-faithful learned-query
Perceiver does not train from a random, a warm, or a centred warm init on LLaVA@66K with a LoRA LLM. The remaining untested cause
is (c)/schedule length (backlog item 5), which the direction's rule reserves for the case that item 2 also failed; it did not.
