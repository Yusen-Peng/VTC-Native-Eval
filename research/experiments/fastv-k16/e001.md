## Interpretation
Cost is settled: FastV-16 and PyramidDrop-16 at 2B are equal-cost to within 0.2 % in FLOPs and 1 % in latency at
every ratio, so e002's DocVQA/MMBench comparison is a pure ranking-quality comparison at fixed cost. FastV-16
is 1.4x / 2.2x / 3.2x the FastV-2 FLOPs at 2x / 4x / 8x, so beating FastV-2 alone would not be a Pareto win at 4x/8x.
The K=16 hook works end to end and costs ~1 pt on RWQA at 2x.
