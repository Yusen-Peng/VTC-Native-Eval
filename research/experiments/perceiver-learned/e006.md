## Interpretation
The e002 fix carries to 8x without collapse, and the loss gain over the pooled architecture at lr 2e-5 is the largest of the
three ratios (-0.059 nats, vs -0.033 at 4x and -0.012 at 2x). Together with e004, the loss gap scales with the compression
ratio, which is what the e003 mechanism predicts (the per-token MLP branch has more to correct the more patches each output
token summarises). On the benchmarks the picture splits: general VQA gains explode with the ratio (MMBench -0.9 / +2.3 / +14.1,
MME +3 / +37 / +177 at 2x / 4x / 8x) while OCR gains stay small and flat (DocVQA +2.7 / +2.0 / +1.1, OCRBench -0.1 / +0.8 / +0.1):
whatever the higher compressor lr lets the module learn restores the coarse image summary the LLM needs for multiple-choice
VQA (the 8x row is now above random pruning 8x on MMBench by 16.5 and MME by 375) but not the fine text content that 8x pooling
destroys (all three Perceiver 8x OCR numbers are at the untrained fixed-pooling floor, within 2 pts). The 8x learned_residual
row beats the pooled row on 7 of 8 benchmarks (RWQA -0.1 is noise); the MMBench and MME predictions, anchored on the small 4x
deltas, missed by a factor of 5-6. Whether the gain is the latents or the lr is e009's question; its first 70 steps (paired
-0.002 vs this family's 4x run, -0.078 vs pooled 4x) say lr, so the row should be read as "Perceiver, pooled-anchored, compressor
lr 1e-3" until e009's gate eval settles the label.
