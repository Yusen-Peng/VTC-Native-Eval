## Interpretation
On the module, as on the loss curve (e009), pooled queries at compressor lr 1e-3 and the learned_residual queries at the
same lr are the same compressor: the same per-sample loss (-0.005, 1.5 SE), the same gain over plain fixed pooling under
their own adapters (-0.178 vs -0.170), the same MLP-gate scale (0.018 vs 0.012; the attention gate is unchanged from init
in both), the same near-one-hot attention, the same 52-56% RMS departure from the fixed-pooling tokens at 85-87% cosine,
and the learned_residual latents at 0.25% of the query with a 0.002-nat effect. The pooled module trained at lr 2e-5 is
fixed pooling (0.7% RMS change, 0.001 nats). The e003 attribution holds at 4x on the module, and more strongly than at
2x: the compressor learns something worth 0.17 nats over fixed pooling here (0.03 at 2x), and the compressor lr is the
only thing that lets it. Alternative explanations ruled out: the latents (zeroing them costs 0.002 nats), the attention
gate (unchanged from init at both lrs), different data or adapters (the lr/fixed and residual/fixed losses agree to
0.003, so the two adapters see fixed pooling the same way).
