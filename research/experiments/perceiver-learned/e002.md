## Hypothesis
Tests causes (a)+(b) together (backlog item 2): a Perceiver whose learned latent queries are a zero-initialised
residual on top of the pooled queries (init = fixed pooling exactly, tanh gates at 0, as in the `pooled` mode that
trained fine) does not collapse, because the LLM receives per-position visual content from step 0 and the latents
get a direct gradient through the output tokens; the mechanism measured in e001 (random unit queries drowned by a
shared O(1) attention residual, output pairwise cosine 0.995 at init) cannot occur when the query identity is the
pooled token itself. Compressor lr is raised to 1e-3 (cause (c) as a possible contributor) so that the latents and
gates can move in 488 steps; at 2e-5 the pooled run's gates ended at 0.004, which would make the mode redundant with
`pooled` by construction.

## Interpretation
The direction's primary criterion is met: a learned-query Perceiver reaches 73.37 MMBench / 40.00 DocVQA at 2x on
LLaVA@66K (gate 72 / 30), and the collapse is explained by e001's measurement (cause (b): random unit queries drowned by
the shared attention residual) plus the fact that removing it by construction (queries start as the pooled tokens, latents
and gates at 0) trains like the pooled module from step 1 and ends slightly better. What the result does NOT yet show is
whether the latents do anything: with compressor lr 1e-3 vs the pooled row's 2e-5 the small loss gap (0.01 nats) and the
DocVQA gain (+2.7) could come from the gated attention/MLP residuals moving more at the higher lr rather than from the
learned query shift. e003 separates the two by ablating the latents and the whole trained module on the same 128 probe
samples, and gives the 2x row its remaining six benchmarks. The resume failures were code bugs in a never-used branch and
say nothing about the hypothesis.
