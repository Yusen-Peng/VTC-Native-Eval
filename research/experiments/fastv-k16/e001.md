## Hypothesis
FastV-16 at 2B costs almost exactly PyramidDrop-16's prefill FLOPs at every ratio (both keep all 4096 visual
tokens through 16 of 40 layers), and the K=16 hook runs end to end in a real eval.

## Prediction (before submission)
Derived from the existing flops.csv per-layer costs (full layer 0.338 TF; FastV-2 rows give a pruned layer of
0.144 / 0.066 / 0.032 TF at 2x/4x/8x): FastV-16 2B TFLOPs 8.91 / 7.04 / 6.22 at 2x/4x/8x, each within 0.05 of
PyramidDrop-16 (8.92 / 7.06 / 6.24): 85 %. LLM latency at 2x between FastV-2 (236 ms) and PyramidDrop-16 (362 ms)
and within 10 % of PyramidDrop-16 when both are re-measured in the same job: 75 %.
Smoke: RealWorldQA at 2B 2x finishes rc=0 in under 15 min, accuracy within 3 pt of baseline 62.74: 80 %.
